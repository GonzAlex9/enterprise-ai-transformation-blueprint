# Prioritization

## Scoring model

Score each use case from 1–5.

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Business value | 30% |
| Feasibility | 20% |
| Data readiness | 15% |
| Risk profile (5 = low/manageable risk) | 15% |
| Time to value | 20% |

**Priority score = weighted average × 20**

## Initial portfolio

| Use case | Value | Feas. | Data | Risk | TTV | Score |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Customer support copilot | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **90** |
| Invoice exception triage | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | **83** |
| Sales proposal assistant | 4 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | **77** |
| Demand forecasting | 5 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 2 | **68** |
| Supplier risk assistant | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | **66** |
| Autonomous purchase approval | 4 | 2 | 3 | 1 | 2 | **52** |

## Recommended sequence

**Wave 1:** customer support copilot + invoice exception triage.

**Wave 2:** sales proposal assistant + supplier risk assistant.

**Separate analytics track:** demand forecasting.

**Do not prioritize:** autonomous purchase approval.

A high-value use case can still be a bad first pilot. Early pilots should optimize for:

> **learning speed × measurable value × manageable risk**
